Introduction to Sangam Management Service

Sangam Management Service is a training and management service institution based in Tinkune, Kathmandu, Nepal, dedicated to preparing prospective migrant workers for safe, informed, skilled, and responsible employment abroad.

Vision

Trusted training institution contributing to safe migration and worker welfare.

Mission

Quality orientation, practical skills, and ethical recruitment advocacy.

Core Offerings

Main Objectives and Programmes

Malaysia PDOT Cluster

Pre-departure orientation and related training for workers travelling to Malaysia under applicable government approvals.

Skill Training

Developing practical and occupational skills that improve workers’ employability, productivity, and workplace safety.

Housemaid Training

Preparing domestic workers in household management, hygiene, safety, communication, and responsible employment.

Caregiver Training

Basic and practical training in personal care, elderly care, daily living assistance, hygiene, and safe caregiving practices.

Management Services

Institutional management support, administrative services, training coordination, and professional consulting.

Online & Other Services

Accessible online information, digital learning support, and market-demand training modules.
